## Project description

Tablekeeper is a restaurant reservation service produced by the Proofline factory in BAND. Three distinct coding seats divide responsibility: the Coordinator transports the complete assignment, the Builder implements and preserves each stage, and the Verifier checks the exact revision independently.

The four services extend the contract in order: atomic and idempotent API operations, the browser booking workflow, effective-dated policies and recurring reservations, and atomic series changes and closure replanning. Each stage includes its own Dockerfile and run instructions. The application needs no external model at runtime.

The Builder repaired validation and the independent recheck approved the corrected revision, including rejection of the invalid import without changing the destination.

The original room, generic mandates and exact-revision reports preserve the collaboration. Operator recovery occurred between stages and during stage four, including one administrative notice. The supervisor made no production-code edits or repeated stage dispatches. We disclose this intervention and do not claim uninterrupted autonomy.
